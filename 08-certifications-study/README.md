## Suggested study approach

1. Read the official exam objectives first — study *to* the objectives, not just a book cover-to-cover
2. Take a diagnostic practice test before studying to find weak domains
3. Build notes per domain (not per chapter) so review sessions are targeted
4. Do hands-on labs where possible (home lab, TryHackMe, Hack The Box) — certifications test applied understanding, not just memorization
5. Practice tests close to exam date to gauge readiness and build time-management for the actual exam

## General exam-day reminders

- Read every question fully before answering — certification exams often include distractor options that sound right but are subtly wrong
- Flag uncertain questions and return to them rather than getting stuck
- For performance-based questions (PBQs), attempt them but don't let them eat all your time — they're often weighted less than the multiple-choice majority
